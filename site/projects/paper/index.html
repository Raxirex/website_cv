The Space of Continuum Mechanisms

The general theorem for static conservative mechanisms, the finished paper on effective joints, and the complete record

Ege Çelikel

The result in four lines. Seen through its ports, a static conservative mechanism is a lower semicontinuous energy on the port space. These energies form a compact metric space in which distance is what spring-loaded probes measure. Connection of mechanisms is lower semicontinuous in that space, and continuous for soft partners. Every point of the space is a limit of linkages of bars, pins and one spring. These statements are checked by machine in Lean 4 with Mathlib. That the same holds for bodies cut from one elastic material is conjectured.
